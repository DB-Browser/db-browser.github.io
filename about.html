About this project

This site focuses on a Windows portable build of DB Browser with SQLCipher support. Our goal is to provide a clean download flow (via CAPTCHA), practical guides, troubleshooting tips and best practices for working with encrypted SQLite databases on Windows.

Get Portable (SQLCipher) FAQ Contact

All downloads are protected by a CAPTCHA gate on the Download page. No direct file links are exposed on this site.

Mission & scope

Mission: Help Windows users work productively with encrypted SQLite databases by packaging a portable SQLCipher‑enabled build and curating practical guidance: getting started, encryption basics, import/export, performance and troubleshooting.

Scope: This site covers Windows, a portable EXE workflow, and SQLCipher‑related topics. For other platforms and advanced usage beyond our guides, please refer to the official project and community documentation.

What we cover / What we don’t

We cover

  • Portable download flow with a CAPTCHA gate
  • Opening existing SQLCipher databases and fixing common errors
  • Encrypting, rekeying and migrating databases
  • CSV import/export and performance tuning

We don’t

  • Publish direct file links on this site
  • Provide official support or warranties
  • Maintain installers or builds for other OS here
